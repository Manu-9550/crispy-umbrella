⚠️ One important change to your original plan

Don't try to finish every vulnerability in existence in 3–4 months.

Your first target should be:

Become extremely good at understanding HTTP requests and manually testing web applications.

For example, if you see:

GET /api/order/83921 HTTP/1.1
Host: target.example
Cookie: session=...

you should automatically start thinking:

Authentication?
Authorization?
Can I access another object's data?
Can I change HTTP method?
Can I modify parameters?
Is there another API endpoint?
What happens without the cookie?
What happens with another authorized account?
Does the frontend expose additional functionality?

That thinking is what turns you into a bug hunter.

And don't test random real websites. For the real-bounty stage, use only programs whose scope explicitly authorizes your testing; OWASP WSTG is designed around professional web-security testing methodology.
